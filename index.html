How to use this app (Important)

The Band 8 Rule

Improve fast by focusing on ONE thing per answer. Don’t try to fix everything at once.

  • Pronunciation: stress / endings / linking
  • Vocabulary: collocations + paraphrase
  • Grammar: accuracy + 1–2 “switches”
  • Fluency: fewer long pauses

Timers

  • Part 1: speak for 35 seconds
  • Part 2: 1 minute plan + 2 minutes speak
  • Part 3: speak for 60 seconds
  • Fluency Gym: 3-speed training (30s each)

Recording

You can record Part 2 answers. Allow microphone access. Recording works best on HTTPS (Vercel is HTTPS ✅).

Submit to Teacher

The app can send your current task + notes to the teacher using /api/submit. If Telegram env vars are enabled on Vercel, it will forward to Telegram automatically.

  • It submits: Part + Topic + Current question (+ Part 2 notes)
  • Your Name/Group/Level are included

Privacy

Your notes and profile are saved only in your browser. The teacher receives only what you submit.
